HOLD THE FRONT PAGE: TEACHER VIEW

Students open , type their real name and a display name, and tap their table letter. This page refreshes every 3 seconds. Each table plays one match at a time (2 to 10 players, always split into two equal teams, max 5 a side; with an odd number one player sits out and plays next round).

Start now is the ONLY way a round begins (READY just shows who is set; works from 2 players). Reset sends the table back to its lobby. Pick a mode per table (it applies from the next round): Fact/Opinion, Tabloid/Broadsheet, or one of two 4-desk device sets.
